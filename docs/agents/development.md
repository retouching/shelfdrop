# Development

The rules of the code are in `CLAUDE.md`; this page describes the code as it is.

## Module map
- `shelfdrop/main.py`: `create_app()` (the uvicorn factory: reads `Settings`, configures logging, creates the data directory) and `build_app(settings)` (routers, middlewares, error handlers, the pages, the container, and the lifespan, which imports the settings of the environment at start).
- `shelfdrop/config.py`: `Settings`.
- `shelfdrop/container.py`: `injectables(settings)`; `open_http` (the `httpx.AsyncClient` of the sources, with the User-Agent of `user_agent`), `new_work_sources` (the metadata clients, in the order of `unavailable`), `new_secret_box`, `new_settings_reader`, `environment_values` (the variables imported once into the settings).
- `shelfdrop/cli/main.py`: the `shelfdrop` command; `shelfdrop db upgrade` applies the migrations, `shelfdrop db check` compares the database with the models (exit 1 on a difference), `shelfdrop placement run` runs the placement worker (`shelfdrop.container.run_placement`, with the umask `002`).
- `shelfdrop/db/`: `base.py` (`Base` and its naming convention), `engine.py` (`new_engine`, which sets WAL, the foreign keys and a 5 second wait on every SQLite connection; `open_engine`, `open_session`), `migrate.py` (`upgrade_database`, `check_database`), `models/` (`SourceAnswer`, `CachedWork`, `ReleaseAnswer`, `Tracker`, `FilterRule`, `Setting`, `Secret`, `AuditEntry`, `LoginSession`, `InternalAccount`, `ApiToken`, `Download`, `Event`, `ShelfFile`, `LibraryTask`, `PlaceItem`, `WorkMerge`, `Upload`), `migrations/` (Alembic, batch mode for SQLite).
- `shelfdrop/works/`:

  - `repository.py`: `WorkRepository`, the cache (`read_answers`, `save_answers`, `save_works`, `read_work`, `remove_expired`, `read_merges`, `add_merge`, `delete_merges`, `commit`).
  - `flights.py`: `SearchFlights` and `SourceHealth`, container singletons.

## Layers
A router calls its service and maps its domain exceptions to HTTP errors; a service holds the rules and calls its repository and its clients; a repository runs the queries and commits. `tests/unit/test_layers.py` checks the imports: the domain never imports a framework nor the HTTP layer, wireup stays in the composition and the routers, the routers never touch the database, the utilities import the standard library alone.

The two routes of the work search are `async`: the search waits on every source at once on the event loop, and its service runs the repository in the threadpool ([ADR 0007](../adr/0007-work-search.md)).

## Database
Caches: `source_answers` (the candidates a source gave for a family and a normalized text, a day), `cached_works` (a merged work by its id, a week), `work_merges` (the works the page merged by hand: the keys of their members, the name chosen, [ADR 0007](../adr/0007-work-search.md)) and `release_answers` (what Prowlarr gave for a name, its categories and its indexers, 10 minutes). Settings: `trackers` (the indexers of Prowlarr, their choices, their order, the categories Prowlarr gives for each and those chosen by kind, JSON, null when never chosen), `filter_rules` (in their order) and `settings` (a JSON value by key: `filters.minimum`, the settings of the page; who changed it last). `secrets` holds the sealed secrets of the services, `audit` who changed which setting and when, never a value ([ADR 0012](../adr/0012-settings-and-secrets.md)). `sessions` holds the sessions of the accounts signed in, by the SHA-256 of their token, and `internal_account` the internal account, one row at most: its name, the scrypt of its password and the end of its set-up ([ADR 0013](../adr/0013-sign-in-and-sessions.md), [ADR 0024](../adr/0024-first-launch-setup.md)). `downloads` holds the releases sent to qBittorrent: their torrent and its id, their download link without the key of Prowlarr, their work, author, series, volumes, kind, mode and category, their stage (`sending`, `sent`, `completed`, `failed` and its reason), their attempts, who sent them and when ([ADR 0014](../adr/0014-downloads.md)). `events` holds the events of the journal, their group, their facts (JSON), their picture and the state of their message ([ADR 0015](../adr/0015-journal.md)). A download placed keeps its destination, one left to Ranger its reason (`unplaced`), one being merged its percent; `files` holds the files the placement put on the shelf (their work, series, author, volume or chapter, path, and whether Jellyfin holds their metadata locked), `library_tasks` the `cover.jpg` the API asks the worker to write and the scans of the libraries ([ADR 0016](../adr/0016-placement.md)); `place_items` the files of Ranger, their files, chapters, the chapters loose in the folder of their series, state, request and error ([ADR 0018](../adr/0018-ranger.md)); `uploads` the files sent from the page Manuel, their chunks received, their SHA-256 and their state ([ADR 0019](../adr/0019-uploads.md)). `api_tokens` holds the access tokens of the API and of the MCP server, by the SHA-256 of their secret, their scope, expiry, last use and revocation ([ADR 0020](../adr/0020-access-tokens.md)). The rows of the caches are JSON written and read by `WorkRepository` through Pydantic `TypeAdapter`s of the types of `shelfdrop.works.types`.

## Integration tests
`tests/integration/conftest.py` starts one stack per session, `shelfdrop-test-<random>`, from `docker/compose.yaml` and `docker/compose.test.yaml`: the real image of the API, its loopback port drawn at random, and `fake-sources`, WireMock 3.13.2, which the API reaches on the network `sources`, with no way out. Prowlarr is played by it too (`SHELFDROP_PROWLARR_URL` and `SHELFDROP_PROWLARR_API_KEY`, imported into the settings at start, `tests.integration.support.prowlarr_stubs`, from `tests/unit/releases/fixtures/prowlarr.json`). The API sends every request of the metadata clients there (`SHELFDROP_SOURCES_ORIGIN`, `shelfdrop.works.clients.redirect.RedirectTransport`), its first origin in `X-Original-Origin`; the session loads into WireMock one stub per exchange recorded in `tests/unit/works/fixtures/` (`tests.integration.support.recorded_stubs`), a recorded failure replayed as a reset connection, and a request recorded with no stub gets a `404`. A failed source is not skipped there (`SHELFDROP_SOURCE_REST_SECONDS=0`), so that no test disturbs the next one. A torrent sent is looked for every second there (`SHELFDROP_SEND_CHECK_SECONDS=1`), and a batch of the journal waits 2 seconds (`SHELFDROP_JOURNAL_BATCH_SECONDS=2`); the webhook of Discord is played by it too (`discord_stub`, saved by the fixture `webhook_saved`; `wait_for_messages` reads the messages posted). The service `placement` runs too, a round every second (`SHELFDROP_PLACEMENT_EVERY_SECONDS=1`), on a volume `media` of its own on `/data`: the fixture `media` makes its folders, the tests make their files there (`stack.exec_placement`, ffmpeg among them) and play the libraries of Jellyfin (`jellyfin_library_stubs`; `jellyfin_saved` saves its key); `send_completed` sends a release and completes it in qBittorrent. A real Deluge runs too (the service `deluge`, the image of linuxserver by digest, on the network `sources` alone, the volume `media` on `/data`): `tests/integration/test_torrent_deluge.py` drives its Web UI from the container of the API (`deluge_calls`). Jellyfin is played by it too (`SHELFDROP_JELLYFIN_URL`, `tests.integration.support.jellyfin_stubs`: an administrator and an account that is not one, the server and its libraries under the key of the tests), and qBittorrent behind qui (`qbittorrent_stubs`, under the address the tests type or the fixture `torrent_saved` saves; `torrent_stubs`, a torrent in a WireMock scenario of its own, on its hash alone: listed once added, lost once, complete on a request of the test; a list of several hashes goes to the service `qbittorrent-lists`, `tests/integration/qbittorrent_lists.py`, which asks WireMock for each hash and joins the torrents, as qBittorrent lists them, F33; `FakeSources.requests` leaves its requests out), and the torrents of Prowlarr (`prowlarr_release`, `prowlarr_download_stub`, built by `tests.unit.support.torrent_file`). A real Transmission runs too (the service `transmission`, the image of linuxserver pinned, its credentials of the tests, on the network `sources` alone and on the volume `media`, as the user of the worker): `tests/integration/test_torrent_transmission.py` saves it as the client (`transmission_saved`) and asks its RPC from its container. The fixture `session_token` signs in once per run; `client` sends its cookie and the `Origin` of the pages (httpx keeps no `Secure` cookie over plain HTTP), `anonymous` sends no cookie. The service `qbittorrent` of `docker/compose.test.yaml` (the profile `torrent-qbittorrent`, started by `tests/integration/test_torrent_qbittorrent.py` alone, removed at its end) is a real qBittorrent, its WebUI account set by `docker/qbittorrent/qBittorrent.conf`, saving into the volume `media` of the placement worker, its port published on the loopback for the tests. The service `api-setup` of `docker/compose.test.yaml` (the profile `setup`, started by `tests/integration/test_setup.py` alone, removed at its end) is a second API on a database in memory, without Prowlarr nor Jellyfin in its environment: the set-up of the first launch from its start. `tests/integration/support.py` holds `Stack` (its logs, `exec_api`), `FakeSources` (load stubs, count requests, list the unmatched ones), `compose`, `sign_in` and `signed_in_headers`.
