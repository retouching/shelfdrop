# 0018. Ranger: the files placed by hand
Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-10-24

## Context
Decided on 2026-10-21: what the placement worker does not place alone (a manual download, a release in chapters, a volume it cannot read, a link refused) and the files of the libraries out of their names go to the page Ranger. There a file is linked to a work (the work search) and placed by the same rules as the worker; a release in chapters is linked to its real volumes by hand (MangaDex is wrong on Chainsaw Man, ADR 0016), a complete volume merged into one CBZ, an incomplete one placed as it is. Nothing is replaced: a file at the destination refuses the request. A scan, every day and on a button, finds the files misplaced, the folders of chapters and the MP3 tracks not merged already on the shelf.

## Decision
- The files of Ranger are the rows of `place_items` (`shelfdrop.db.models.place_item`): their origin (`download`, `library`), their files (the paths of the worker), their size, their state (`unlinked`, `chapters`, `misplaced`, `placed`, `ignored`), why they are there, their chapters, the series their download or their folder gives and its volumes on the shelf.
- The API never touches the files (ADR 0016): `RangerService` (`shelfdrop/ranger/`) lists the rows, keeps the request of the page on a row (`request`, JSON of `shelfdrop.placement.types.PlaceRequest`) and asks for a scan (a row `scan` of `library_tasks`); `HandPlacement` (`shelfdrop/placement/byhand.py`), in each round of the worker after `PlacementService.round`, does the rest.
- **Listed.** Each download `unplaced`, and each manual download `completed`, gets its rows once (`shelfdrop.placement.items.items_of`, its files read in the `content_path` the torrent client gives, but those it put on the shelf already: a file of the torrent that has the inode of a file of `files` of the download, its hardlink, or that a file of `files` names as the file it is a copy of (`copied_from`), `shelfdrop.placement.byhand.not_shelved`; a download placed in part, ADR 0016, lists its rest alone): a release in chapters one row `chapters` with every chapter of its names (`Ch. 233-236` counts 233 to 236, `.5` kept); another manga or a book one row a book file; an audiobook one row a folder of tracks.
- **A volume** (`VolumeRequest`): a manga `<Series> - TXX.<ext>` (`shelfdrop.placement.chapters.place_volume`), a book and an audiobook by `place_books` and `lay_audio` (the title given, or the series; MP3 tracks merged into one M4B). **A file of chapters** (`ChapterRequest`): `<Series> - T23 - Ch. 206` or `<Series> - Ch. 233-236`. A file of a download is hardlinked (the torrent seeds on), or copied as `placement.mode` says (ADR 0016); a file of the libraries is moved (linked, then its old name removed; copied then removed only when the link is refused and the mode is not `hardlink`). A name taken: `exists`, nothing written; the row stays with its `error`.
- **A release in chapters** (`ChaptersRequest`, `place_chapters`): each volume is a range of its real chapters, even those the release lacks. Complete (every whole chapter of the range in the release or loose in the folder of the series; the worker reads the loose ones again at each round for each release in chapters left in Ranger, `place_items.loose`, so that the page counts a volume complete as the placement will): merged into one CBZ (`merge_chapters`: the first page of its first chapter as its cover, `ComicInfo.xml` with the series, the number, the writers and the summary), and the loose chapters of the shelf it took removed (the click is the confirmation). Incomplete: its chapters linked as they are, `<Series> - TNN - Ch. NNN`. A volume on the shelf is left, but replaced when the page asks (`replace`). The chapters of no volume: placed as they are (`<Series> - Ch. NNN`, `rest` `place`), or kept in Ranger (`keep`: the row keeps them). The cover of a volume is the first page of its first chapter: no source gives one cover a volume we trust.
- **Placed**: the files in `files`, the row `placed` with its destination (`copied` when a file of its download was copied, « copié » on the page), the download `placed` once none of its rows is open (and tagged `placé`), the library scanned by Jellyfin, the event `placed`. The lock of the round locks them, a chapter after the volumes before it.
- **Ignored** (`IgnoreRequest`, `{"type": "ignore"}`, after a confirmation on the page): the worker makes the row `ignored` and touches no file, of a download, of the libraries or sent alike (a file sent ignored stays in the folder of the uploads: `uploads.keep_days` removes only the files still to place). The row leaves the count and the files to place (the page shows it under « Ignorés »), its download is `placed` once none of its rows is open, with the destination of its last row placed, else the series of a file the worker placed alone, else none; no event `placed`. The scan keeps an ignored file of the libraries ignored (it is not listed again), and removes its row once the file is gone. `POST /api/v1/place/items/{id}/resume` (« Reprendre ») puts the row back at once, by the API: `chapters` when it holds chapters, `misplaced` from the libraries, `unlinked` otherwise; its download, placed meanwhile, stays `placed` (and tagged): the row is placed from Ranger as any other.
- **The scan** (`shelfdrop.placement.scan.scan_library`): a book file at the root of a library (`root`); a manga volume whose name is not `<Series> - TXX` in the folder of its series, a file deeper, a book out of `<Author>/<Series>/<NN - Title>/` (`naming`); the chapter files of a series folder (`chapters`, one row with the volumes of the folder); a folder of two MP3 tracks or more without an M4B when `placement.merge_mp3` is on (`tracks`). What the placement put on the shelf (`files`) is left aside. New rows added, those gone removed, the others read again (but those with a request). It runs once a day at `placement.scan_time` (local time of the worker), and when the page asks; the new files go to the journal (`misplaced`, a count a library).
- **A file of the shelf corrected**: `GET /api/v1/place/items?q=` lists the files of `files` whose path holds the text; `POST /api/v1/place/shelf/{id}/link` makes it a row of the libraries with its request: it is moved.

## Rejected alternatives
- The files listed by the API: it does not see the libraries nor the torrents (no mount), and must not.
- A cover a volume from MangaDex: not reliable (ADR 0016); the first page of the first chapter, as the page says.
- A copy of a file of the libraries: a move keeps one file; a hardlink then the removal of the old name never replaces anything.
- An ignored file removed: a file of a download seeds on, and it may be wanted back.
- An ignored file taken back by the worker, its download made `unplaced` again: a mark `placé` to remove from the torrent client and an event for a file put aside by hand; the row comes back at once, its download stays placed.
- The placed files of a download listed again by their path: `files` keeps their path on the shelf, not in the torrent; their inode is the one of their hardlink.

## Consequences
- A request waits for the next round of the worker (30 seconds at most); the page shows `placing` until then.
- The daily scan follows the time zone of the worker (`TZ`).
- A file of the libraries placed from Ranger leaves its old name; a file of a download keeps it (the torrent seeds).
- A file of a torrent the shelf holds through something else than a hardlink (the M4B merged from its MP3 tracks) shares no inode with it: listed again, it is refused (`exists`) or ignored.
