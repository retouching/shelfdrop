# 0016. The placement of the downloads, by a worker of its own
Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-10-24

## Context
Decided on 2026-10-21: an automatic download complete is placed alone, the testable rewrite of the scripts that did it before shelfdrop: a hardlink in the library (the torrent seeds on), `<Series> - TXX` for a manga, `<Author>/<Series>/<NN - Title>/<Series> - TNN - <Title>.<ext>` for a book, a cover, the MP3 tracks of an audiobook merged into one M4B with its chapters and its cover, Jellyfin scanned, then the name, the series, the number and the authors set and locked. A worker of its own, `worker-placement`, is the one process that writes in the libraries. Before any import, the shelf is compared: only what is missing is added, a volume there is never replaced; a release in chapters is never placed alone (Ranger, by hand).

## Decision
- `shelfdrop placement run` (the service `placement` of Compose, the same image) runs `PlacementService.round` every 30 seconds: the downloads `sent` qBittorrent has all of are completed (as the page does; `mark_completed` completes each once); each automatic download `completed` is placed; the files placed are looked for in Jellyfin and locked; the writes the API asked for are done.
- The files of a torrent are the `content_path` qBittorrent gives (as it sees them: the worker mounts the same folder on `/data`).
- A manga (`shelfdrop.placement.layout.place_comics`): the volume of each book is read in its name (`shelfdrop.placement.naming.file_volume`); a release of one file without a number is its volume. The books whose volume it cannot read are left (`Laid.unknown`), the others placed: the download is then placed in part, `unplaced` (`no_volume`) with the event `to_place` alone (no `placed`, no tag `placé`), its files placed in `files`, the library scanned; Ranger lists the rest alone and places the download once the rest is linked or ignored (ADR 0018). None read: `no_volume`, nothing placed. The folder of the series is read first (`existing.content_of`): a volume there is left (`present`, in the journal: « Déjà présents »). A release in chapters (most of its files name a chapter and no volume, or far more files than its volumes) is `unplaced` (`chapters`). The cover of the series, without one, is the first page of its first volume (`cover.<ext>`); a PDF gets `<volume>.jpg` (pdftoppm).
- A book: the volume of the page for one volume; in a pack, the volume its folder or its name states, the files without one left to Ranger as for a manga (placed in part); its title read in the name of the release, after its number and before its tags, else the series. A volume whose folder holds a book is left. Without an author: `unknown_author`.
- An audiobook: a track without a volume leaves the whole of it to Ranger (`no_volume`: its tracks go by folder); one M4B is linked; MP3 tracks are linked as `... - 001.mp3`, merged by the ffmpeg of the image (`shelfdrop.placement.audio`: one chapter a track, AAC 96 kbit/s, `+faststart`, `.part` renamed once whole), with the picture of the torrent, else the one of the tracks (`folder`), else the cover of the work at its sources (`shelfdrop.works.covers.WorkCovers`); the tracks of the shelf are removed once merged, the torrent keeps its own. The percent of the merge goes to the page. A merge that fails leaves the tracks and is `merge_failed`.
- Nothing is replaced: a hardlink or a file never overwrites (`os.link` refuses an existing target), a merge writes into a `.part`. A link the file system refuses (another file system, no right) is `link_refused`; the files placed before stay, and are kept.
- Placed: the files in `files`, the download `placed` with its destination (« BD & Mangas › Berserk »: the library of Jellyfin of the kind, found by the name of its folder, `shelfdrop.placement.paths`), the tag `placé` in qBittorrent, the library scanned (`POST /Items/{library}/Refresh`, the new files), and the event `placed` with the cover. Not placed: `unplaced` with its reason, the event `to_place`, and `failed` for a link refused or a merge failed.
- The lock (`shelfdrop.placement.metadata.locked_item`), once Jellyfin lists the file (24 hours at most): `Name`, `SortName`, `ForcedSortName` (the name of the file; a chapter alone sorts after the volumes before it), `SeriesName`, `IndexNumber`, the authors (`People` of type `Author`) when it has none, `LockedFields` `Name` and `Cast`; the rest of the item goes back as it was read.
- The API never writes in the libraries: the `cover.jpg` of a series chosen on the page Jellyfin is a row of `library_tasks` (the folder as Jellyfin sees it, the bytes) the worker writes (`replace_with`).
- The sync asked from the page Jellyfin (decided on 2026-10-24; `shelfdrop.library.sync.LibrarySync`): the lock again, for the files of `files` of a series or of every library, through the API of Jellyfin alone (the API still never touches a file). Each file Jellyfin lists by its path gets `locked_item` in its forced mode: the authors of shelfdrop replace those of the item (its other people kept), its image is never sent (the image of a volume stays the one Jellyfin read in its file). It runs whatever `placement.lock_jellyfin` says (the sync is asked), in the background, one at a time; its state and its counts stay in memory, and the files it reached are marked locked.
- The file of a volume downloaded from the page goes through Jellyfin (`GET /Items/{id}/Download`, streamed), for a book or an audiobook of a library of a kind alone: the API mounts no library.
- The merge of chapters into one volume (`shelfdrop.placement.comics.merge_chapters`: the cover first, the pages of each chapter in order numbered again, a `ComicInfo.xml` with the series, the number, the writers, the summary and the pages, the cover declared `FrontCover`) and the volumes complete of a range of chapters (`existing.complete_volumes`) are the rules Ranger calls.

## Rejected alternatives
- The placement in the API: the API would write in the libraries, and a merge of 15 minutes would run in the process of the pages.
- A copy when a hardlink fails: the space of the torrent twice, unseen.
- MangaDex for the chapters of each volume: wrong on Chainsaw Man (13 volumes of 24, a chapter in the wrong volume, 2026-10-21): the chapters are linked to their volumes by hand.
- `docker exec jellyfin` for ffmpeg: the worker would need the socket of Docker.

## Consequences
- A download complete is placed within 30 seconds; the page shows `placing` until then (and the percent of a merge).
- The libraries of Jellyfin must be named by their folders as the worker names them.
- A sync never adds the files placed by hand outside shelfdrop: they are not in `files`; a restart of the API forgets the state of the last sync.
- A volume of a series kept under another name is not seen as there: it is placed again under its name of the shelf.
